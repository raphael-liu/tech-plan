## Corrections and boundaries

- Page 14 of 《技术管理者的角色职责》 defines leading indicators as actions influencing goal attainment and lagging indicators as outcome measurements. Its example table places launch dates, incident rates, and TP99 under leading indicators, while weekly integration, optimization, and drills appear under lagging indicators, contradicting those definitions. The skill uses definitions and actual predictive relationships instead of copying the table's classification. Activity frequency alone does not establish predictive value; explain the causal relationship.
- Example team sizes, durations, thresholds, incident levels, and a one-week escalation window are contextual examples, not universal defaults.
- Asking WHY five times does not guarantee a root cause. One-way doors warrant caution rather than earlier execution by default.
- Technical solutions cannot independently fix every organizational problem. The 5B options—development, retention/incentives, borrowing, hiring, and workforce restructuring—are planning alternatives, not authorization for personnel actions.
- Rollouts and rollback apply to some technical changes. Research and organizational capability initiatives need pilots, validation, exit, or compensation; not every decision is reversible.
